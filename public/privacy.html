Privacy Policy for Mohammed Saqlain's Apps — Money Tracker

Effective Date: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mohammed Saqlain's Apps ("we", "us", or "our"), creator of Money Tracker, collects, protects, uses, and handles your information when you use our application.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
Money Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information We Collect and Store

Money Tracker is architected as an offline-first, private personal bookkeeping application:

  • Financial Data (Local-First): Accounts, balances, transactions, recurring bills, budget targets, notes, and audit logs are recorded on your device within browser storage (IndexedDB and LocalStorage). We do not transmit or sell your financial transactions to third parties.
  • Google Account Identity: If you sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID) provided by Google Identity Services to authenticate your identity.

2. Google Drive Permissions and Data Use

When you enable Cloud Backup or Restore with Google Drive, the application requests access to the following scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files that you use with this app).

How we use Google Drive data:

  • Application-Created Files Only: Money Tracker only accesses, writes, and reads the backup file(s) that it explicitly creates (e.g., money_tracker_backup.json). It has no access to your private photos, Google Docs, Sheets, or other personal documents on your Google Drive.
  • User-Initiated: Backups to Google Drive and restores from Google Drive are only executed when you explicitly press the Backup or Restore buttons.
  • No Human Viewing or Data Sharing: Your Google Drive files and financial backup contents are never reviewed by humans, never transferred to advertising networks, and never used for machine learning or profiling.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data or Google Drive files to external parties. Your data remains strictly between your device and your personal Google account.

4. Data Retention, Export, and Deletion

  • Local Data: You can export your full data anytime in JSON, CSV, or Excel formats, or completely clear your local storage from the in-app settings.
  • Google Drive Backups: You can delete any backup files created by Money Tracker at any time directly through Google Drive or via the app's backup management tool.

5. Security

All network communications between the application, Firebase Authentication, and Google APIs occur over TLS/HTTPS encryption.
